# Your privacy, in plain words

- We keep your email, username and birthday so your account works.
- Your profile and leaderboard scores are private unless your parent or guardian says otherwise.
- Your parent or guardian can see where you are signed in and what games you play, but **not** what you say or make.
- You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

If anything worries you, talk to a grown-up you trust or contact {{support_email}}.
