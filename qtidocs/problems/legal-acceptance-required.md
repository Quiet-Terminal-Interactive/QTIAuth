# LEGAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/legal-acceptance-required`
- `LEGAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED`
- `403`, `422`

## Meaning

## What to do

Present the user with the legal document(s) and collect acceptance before retrying.
