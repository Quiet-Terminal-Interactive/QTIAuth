# INVALID_ACCOUNT_CHOICE

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/invalid-account-choice`
- `INVALID_ACCOUNT_CHOICE`
- `400`

## Meaning

An invalid account was selected during a social sign-in flow where multiple accounts were available.

## What to do

Restart the social sign-in flow and select a valid account.
