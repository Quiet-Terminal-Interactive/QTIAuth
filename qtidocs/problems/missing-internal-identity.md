# MISSING_INTERNAL_IDENTITY

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/missing-internal-identity`
- `MISSING_INTERNAL_IDENTITY`
- `401`

## Meaning

The required internal identity header is absent.

## What to do

Ensure the service includes its internal identity header when calling this endpoint.
