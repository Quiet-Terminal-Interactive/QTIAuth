# APPEAL_CATEGORY_REQUIRED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/appeal-category-required`
- `APPEAL_CATEGORY_REQUIRED`
- `403`

## Meaning

Restricted accounts may only open appeal tickets, not general enquiries.

## What to do

Ensure the user submits an appeal ticket rather than a general support request.
