# PUBLIC_CLIENT_HAS_NO_SECRET

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/public-client-has-no-secret`
- `PUBLIC_CLIENT_HAS_NO_SECRET`
- `409`

## Meaning

Public OAuth clients cannot have a client secret.

## What to do

Remove the client secret or change the client to confidential.
