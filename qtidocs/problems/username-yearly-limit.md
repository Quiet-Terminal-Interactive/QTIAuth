# USERNAME_YEARLY_LIMIT

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/username-yearly-limit`
- `USERNAME_YEARLY_LIMIT`
- `429`

## Meaning

The yearly username change limit has been reached.

## What to do

Inform the user they cannot change their username again until the next year.
