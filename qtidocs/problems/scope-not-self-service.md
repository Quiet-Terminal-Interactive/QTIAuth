# SCOPE_NOT_SELF_SERVICE

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/scope-not-self-service`
- `SCOPE_NOT_SELF_SERVICE`
- `422`

## Meaning

The scope is hidden, game-client-only, or service-only and cannot be self-registered.

## What to do

Contact the platform to have this scope granted to your client.
