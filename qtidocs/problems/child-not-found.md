# CHILD_NOT_FOUND

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/child-not-found`
- `CHILD_NOT_FOUND`
- `404`

## Meaning

The child account was not found in the parental management context.

## What to do

Verify the child's user ID and that the caller is their guardian.
