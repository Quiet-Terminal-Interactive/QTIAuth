# IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/identity-unavailable`
- `IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE`
- `503`

## Meaning

The identity service is temporarily unavailable.

## What to do

Retry after a short delay. Check identity service health if the error persists.
