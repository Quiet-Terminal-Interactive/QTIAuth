# CLIENT_SUSPENDED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/client-suspended`
- `CLIENT_SUSPENDED`
- `400`, `401`

## Meaning

The OAuth client (application) has been suspended and cannot be used.

## What to do

Contact the platform to find out why the client was suspended.
