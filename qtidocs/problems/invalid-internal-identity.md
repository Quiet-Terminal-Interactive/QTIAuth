# INVALID_INTERNAL_IDENTITY

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/invalid-internal-identity`
- `INVALID_INTERNAL_IDENTITY`
- `401`

## Meaning

The internal service identity JWT failed signature verification.

## What to do

Ensure the service is using a valid, unexpired internal identity token.
