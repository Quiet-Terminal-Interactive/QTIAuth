# ACCOUNT_BANNED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/account-banned`
- `ACCOUNT_BANNED`
- `403`

## Meaning

The account is suspended or banned and cannot perform this action.

## What to do

Inform the user their account is restricted. Direct them to appeal if appropriate.
