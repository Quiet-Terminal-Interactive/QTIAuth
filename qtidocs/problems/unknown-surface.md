# UNKNOWN_SURFACE

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/unknown-surface`
- `UNKNOWN_SURFACE`
- `400`, `404`

## Meaning

The request surface (delegation target) is not recognized.

## What to do

Check that the surface identifier is correct and that the surface is configured.
