# PERMISSION_DENIED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/permission-denied`
- `PERMISSION_DENIED`
- `403`

## Meaning

The session does not have the required permission for this action.

## What to do

Verify the user's role and permissions. Do not retry without elevated credentials.
