# WEBAUTHN_CHALLENGE_EXPIRED

## Code

- `https://qtiauth.qtidocs.dev/problems/webauthn-challenge-expired`
- `WEBAUTHN_CHALLENGE_EXPIRED`
- `400`

## Meaning

The WebAuthn challenge has expired, or does not match the expected purpose or user.

## What to do

Restart the WebAuthn flow to obtain a fresh challenge.
