# Integrating

- [HTTP API](/integrating/api): conventions every client shares, such as authentication, errors, rate limits and OpenAPI.
- [Apps (OAuth/OIDC)](/integrating/apps): add "sign in with" to websites, mobile apps, desktop apps and devices.
- [Games](/integrating/games): player sign-in, achievements, stats, leaderboards, playtime, licensing, cloud saves, Steam and safety reports.
